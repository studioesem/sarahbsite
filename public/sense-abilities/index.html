Sense-Abilities as Futures-Making Methods

feeling futures cities

7 December 2026
11:00–16:45RMIT University, 008.04.011 Megaflex 1

Theme

There is a long history of research and creative practice into urban sensing, across: digital sensing, human sensing, asking how the city feels to other species, and sensory making. This playful ‘making’ workshop creates new dialogues between diverse abilities in city sensing, orients them towards possible futures cities, and creates - in one day - a collective outcome which will be shared; a blog post/ sensory device / shared statement / whatever emerges.

This workshop brings together experts in city sensing from different disciplines and with diverse practices.

This event is about using our senses, not simply talking and discussing. So bring your walking shoes, comfortable clothes, a thing (or two) you like to make with at the moment. We will likely be moving and making.

Outline

To ground the creative activities we engage in, we each begin with a 10 minute (max!) summary or demonstration that illustrates our existing practice and methods - this won’t represent the entirety of who we are what we do and what sense-abilities we bring to making city futures, but will simply get us connected to each other and bring energy into this space.

Then we brainstorm to make a decision about what we will create together. (keeping in mind this is small as we have little time)

As questions to pose throughout the day, come with ideas in response to:

  • What does it mean to sense city futures?
  • What will it feel like to co-inhabit possible futures cities with other species and emerging technologies?
  • What theories and what creative practices support our agenda?
  • What intersections might lead to shared sense-abilities?
