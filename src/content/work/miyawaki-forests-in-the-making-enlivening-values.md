note: "In recent years growing numbers of fast-growing \u2018mini forests\u2019 have been planted around the world using an approach for rapid urban greening known as the \u2018Miyawaki method\u2019. Originating in Japan, the Miyawaki method was first developed as a relatively novel ecological engineering approach to the afforestation of industrial and degraded landscapes. However, in recent years escalating climate impacts and loss of biodiversity has inspired a new generation of Miyawaki forest practitioners working globally in diverse ecological contexts."
