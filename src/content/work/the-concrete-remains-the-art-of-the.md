note: "The identity of suburbia, so far as it can be ascribed one, is shifting and insecure, a borderline and liminal space. Dominant stereotypes have listed it as \u2018on the margins\u2019 beyond edges of cultural sophistication and tradition\u2019 and the areas that make up \u2018sprawl\u2019. But in the twenty-first century this static view has to be modified. As is evident from this collection, suburban dwellers themselves have redefined themselves. This collection explores the range and complexity of twenty-first century responses to city suburbs, predominantly in Sydney."
