# Impeccable — Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
The site is the personal home of **Dr. Sarah Barns** — public historian, creative producer, founder of Studio ESEM, and an RMIT Vice Chancellor's Senior Research Fellow working on civic AI governance, participatory storytelling, and place-making technology.

Visitors are: prospective clients and collaborators (councils, cultural institutions, universities), fellow researchers, and readers arriving from her Civic Interplay Substack. Their job-to-be-done is to understand *who she is and what she stands for*, read her writing, and decide whether to reach out, subscribe, or commission work. They read on desktop and phone, often a long-form text in a single sitting.

### Brand Personality
Three words: **place-rooted, contemplative, quietly radical.** Literary and care-full, not corporate or tech-triumphalist. The voice resists speed, optimisation, and "the user" as an abstraction; it favours listening, deep time, memory, and acts of care. Emotional goal: calm conviction — the feeling of a well-made book or a museum caption, with a position being staked.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Established system (keep):** light/white editorial ground for the main site; asymmetric hard-left column (max ~740px) with a fixed video banner on the right; **Fira Sans** (headings/labels) + **Merriweather** (serif body, weight 300); justified, hyphenated prose.
- **Palette:** ESEM brand — ultraviolet `#8c39d8` primary accent, plus teal `#00a69b`, tangerine `#ffaa00`, chartreuse `#d7df23` as category hues.
- **Signature move:** 2px accent border-left on blockquotes (the "zine register"). This is a *deliberate, documented exception* to the usual impeccable ban on >1px side-stripes — preserve it on standard prose pages.
- **Anti-references:** generic AI dark mode (cyan-on-black, purple→blue gradients, neon glow), card-grid templates, hero-metric layouts, gradient text.
- **Showpiece exception — the Manifesto page (`/manifesto`):** a deliberate departure into an **immersive, warm deep-time dark** treatment. Warm near-black soil ground, parchment text, ember/chartreuse (tangerine + chartreuse, both brand hues) as the only accents — ultraviolet intentionally rested here to keep the palette warm. Motif: a single 1px vertical "thread" fading enlightenment→enlivenment, with scroll-lit section nodes. Display face **Young Serif** (organic, rooted) for masthead + headings; Merriweather for body/pull-quotes; Fira Sans for micro-labels. Ragged-right (not justified) for contemplative reading on dark.

### Design Principles
1. **Place over template.** Every page should feel grown from its content, not poured into a card grid. Derive motifs from the writing (thread, resonant traces, deep time), not from UI fashion.
2. **Quiet conviction.** Restraint and generous space over decoration. One orchestrated moment beats many micro-effects. Let typography and rhythm carry the weight.
3. **Warm, never cold.** Tint neutrals and darks toward earth/soil; reserve accents (10%) so they matter. No pure black or white, no neon.
4. **Cohesive departures.** Showpieces may break from the light system, but must stay unmistakably ESEM — same brand hues, same fonts family logic, reuse established motion language (scroll-driven highlights).
5. **Readable first.** Long-form text legibility (line length ≤68ch, added weight/leading for light-on-dark, reduced-motion support) is non-negotiable, even in immersive treatments.
