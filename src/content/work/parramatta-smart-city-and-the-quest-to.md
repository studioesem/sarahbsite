note: "This chapter traces a short profile of Parramatta within the regional and metropolitan strategies of the Greater Sydney area. It highlights how Parramatta evolved from being a suburban centre in Western Sydney \u2013 an area traditionally associated with economic marginality, low employment opportunities and lack of public services such as transport and education facilities \u2013 to becoming one of the fastest-growing metropolitan centres in the country. The chapter analyses the Smart City Masterplan and other technology-driven initiatives that Parramatta undertook between 2010 and 2016."
